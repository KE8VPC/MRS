M.R.S. expanded railroad reference pack - 2026-10-24

467 channel definitions in 8 independent layers. See SOURCES.json for counts and limitations.

Install: extract the railroad-expanded folder into your selected working bandplans folder.
In M.R.S., open Bandplans and click Reload CSV. Disable the old reference/railroad layer
to avoid duplicate baseline markers, then enable the railroad-expanded layers you want.
No executable update or receiver restart is required. No existing CSV files are overwritten.

Use Bandplans > Open folder to locate the selected working directory.

NXDN and analog channel aliases share many exact frequencies. Choose layers by task;
a marker is a published reference, not evidence of local activity. PTC/ITC/ACSES also overlap.
The PTC-7 published base/mobile frequency mismatch is flagged in both records.
The file downlink_mhz column is the generic marker frequency; mobile/MCP rows are not
being claimed as downlinks, and no uplink pairing is inferred. Role and label identify direction.
