# CW station identification alongside digital
Context: CW
Keywords: Morse FCC callsign speed concurrent DMR NXDN

Enable CW station ID alongside digital reception to look for Morse callsign candidates. CW candidates require verification by ear; noise and digital signals can imitate keying. A candidate is not a confirmed owner assignment.

General CW observations appear under Analysis > Messages / CW / inspector. Digital voice status and CW listening can coexist. Tune and filter for the actual signal; CW receive mode is different from detecting an audio-tone station ID on an NFM channel.
