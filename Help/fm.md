# FM broadcast, MPX and stereo pilot
Context: FM
Keywords: WFM RDS RBDS RF audio waveform L R pilot 19 38 57 mono

Broadcast > FM offers channels 201–300 at 88.1–107.9 MHz in 200 kHz increments (47 CFR 73.201). Tune selected applies WFM. A sample rate/capture span must fit the approximately 190 kHz channel filter.

## View selector
RF shows received radio-frequency spectrum. MPX shows the composite spectrum/waterfall: L+R audio at 0–15 kHz, stereo pilot at 19 kHz, L−R at 23–53 kHz and RDS around 57 kHz. Audio (L+R) shows decoded audio spectrum. Waveform L/R shows stereo audio waveforms.

Pilot lock and stereo status describe the stereo decoder. Force mono keeps mono listening even with a pilot. L/R meters show levels. RDS/RBDS selection changes program-type interpretation; station name and radiotext require valid received groups. Missing RDS or stereo can reflect the station/signal. Stop returns the RF view; reference pilot markers do not indicate a detected pilot by themselves.
