# RF squelch and automatic threshold
Context: Squelch
Keywords: trigger threshold noise floor auto scan margin recalibrate gate

RF squelch is on Receiver. Lower thresholds open more easily; higher thresholds reject weaker signals. Auto squelch estimates the noise floor and sets a threshold above it using the margin. Recalibrate after a substantial gain, antenna or interference change.

The Scan trigger and automatic-recording trigger are separate settings. A moving noise floor, continuous control channel, or overly tight margin can change when scanning stops. Use the floor/threshold lines to compare the trigger with real signals. RF squelch controls listening; its state does not prove a decoder is receiving valid frames.
