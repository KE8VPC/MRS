# POCSAG and FLEX paging
Context: Paging
Keywords: capcode label duplicates group ignore payload word wrap

POCSAG/FLEX has its own Public Safety / Business tab. Enable paging, tune accurately and choose a filter that fits the signal. Narrow 512-baud signals may benefit from a 6 kHz receive filter.

Group repeated messages to track duplicates; label capcodes without changing decoded IDs. Ignore rules hide unwanted sources/messages. Show ignored to review them. Select a row for full payload, validation and raw fields. Export preserves the available evidence. Short, weak or incomplete bursts may not produce a complete page.
