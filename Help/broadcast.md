# HD Radio and HDTV
Context: Broadcast
Keywords: ATSC HD nrsc5 digital television setup decoder recording

Broadcast keeps HD Radio and HDTV separate from analog AM/FM. Use each decoder setup/check workflow and sufficient usable capture coverage. Disable capture offset for HD Radio as directed by its setup.

These workflows have distinct capture and decoding requirements. An RF channel marker is only a frequency reference; it is not evidence of ATSC or HD lock. Keep original I/Q and diagnostics when reporting failures. See the bundled HD Radio and HDTV documents for decoder-specific setup details.
