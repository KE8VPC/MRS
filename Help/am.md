# AM broadcast channels
Context: AM
Keywords: broadcast medium wave 530 540 1700 kHz channels filter

Broadcast > AM offers U.S. AM broadcast centers from 540 to 1700 kHz in 10 kHz increments, plus a separately labeled 530 kHz travelers-information reference. These are channel centers, not a station directory. Source: 47 CFR 73.14; supplemental travelers-information reference.

Select a channel and Tune selected. With no running SDR, this stages the controls for Start / Apply. With a running SDR it applies AM mode and its receive filter. The shared spectrum retains its view/history when moving between Spectrum and Broadcast. Hardware must support the selected frequency; standard RTL tuner coverage may require direct sampling or an upconverter for AM.
