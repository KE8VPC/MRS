# P25 systems, voice and site collection
Context: P25
Keywords: NAC WACN system site LCN logical channel bandplan uplink downlink gain

P25 Systems separates site information, channel activity and message inspection. P25 Phase I controls signaling decoding; P25 I voice enables supported voice decoding. The Voice gain slider boosts P25 audio independently of RF gain.

Control announcements provide WACN/system/site identifiers, channel rules and observed grants. LCN means logical channel number, not a date; 01-1968 is a valid channel notation. Uplink/downlink must come from a decoded rule or announcement, not guessed from a nearby frequency.

Bandplans can collect validated live site observations into site folders. Replay data stays separate. Calculated grid markers are not observed channels. A grant/activity row does not mean M.R.S. followed the call. The separate Trunk following tab adds experimental Phase I C4FM group following; live acceptance is pending.
