# Frequency list, editing and labels
Context: Frequencies
Keywords: folder rename edit delete shift ctrl double click tune hide

Single-click selects a frequency or folder. Double-click tunes. Use the right-click menu for Edit, Tune, Delete and folder actions. Ctrl+click toggles entries; Shift selection selects a range. Review the selected entries before bulk deletion.

Labels, notes and grouping are user annotations. Automatic protocol observations preserve your labels. Show/hide controls spectrum markers. An entry can exist even when its marker is hidden. Alt+drag a saved marker to correct its center, then review the updated value. Discovered signals are grouped by detection date.
