# Service overlay expansion after 0.12.0

Implemented in source; existing release ZIPs are unchanged. Enable the U.S. reference bar and service channel markers. ARRL is a separate amateur layer.

- AM: 535–1705 kHz outline; 117 carrier centers, 540–1700 kHz. Source: 47 CFR 73.14, official 2024 annual edition, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title47-vol4/pdf/CFR-2024-title47-vol4.pdf . Current eCFR access was unavailable; reference is dated.
- Aviation: navigation operating area 108–118 MHz (VOR/ILS), communications 118–137 MHz (centers through 136.975), emergency markers 121.5 and 243 MHz. Sources: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/aim_html/chap1_section_1.html ; https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/advisory_circular/ac90-50d.pdf ; https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap6_section_3.html . Operating-area summaries, not exclusive allocations or local airport assignments.
- Marine: selected USCG 06, 09, 13, 16, 70, 1022/22A, duplex 20/24, AIS 1/2. Markers distinguish ship/shore direction and data-only uses. Source reviewed September 29, 2026: https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/us-vhf-channel-information . Broad channel-area bars are envelopes containing gaps and other services, not exclusive marine allocations.

Full marine tables, HF marine, military airband, public-safety/business/TV allocations and the complete FCC table remain unfinished. Reference labels do not add decoder capabilities.
